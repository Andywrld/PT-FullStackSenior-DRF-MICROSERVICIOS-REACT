# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: shoppers browsing and buying from their phone or computer, who want to find a product quickly and check out in a few steps. Secondary: store admins who manage the catalog (products, categories, images) and review orders from a dashboard.

## Product Purpose

A multi-category marketplace (electronics, home, apparel, sports) where a shopper finds a product fast, adds it to the cart and places an order without friction. Success is an order placed in a few clear steps, with the shopper always knowing what they are buying and for how much.

## Positioning

A curated store where the product is the protagonist and buying takes only a few steps.

## Brand Personality

Premium, minimal, calm. The interface steps back so product photography and names carry the page; confidence comes from restraint and precision, not from noise. References: the Apple Store (product-first calm, generous space) and Aesop (quiet, considered, premium without ornament).

## Anti-references

- The default shadcn/ui look, shipped unstyled.
- Saturated marketplaces in the style of Mercado Libre or Amazon: banners everywhere, flashing discounts, visual clutter competing for attention.
- The cream/beige "made by AI" aesthetic.

## Design Principles

- **Product first.** Imagery, name and price lead every screen; chrome and decoration stay out of the way.
- **Calm confidence.** No urgency tricks ("only 2 left!", countdowns, pop-ups). Trust is earned with clarity.
- **Fewer, better choices.** Show less, make it better; every element must earn its place.
- **Every state designed.** Loading, empty and error states are part of the design, never an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus. Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
